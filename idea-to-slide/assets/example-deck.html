The task today
Chasing expert terms, NDAs, contracts, and compliance answers across email
Screening every expert and consultant for negative news before engaging
Reconciling monthly expert-call data and keeping the team's call calendar organized
What the skill does
Shows what you're waiting on, what's in hand, and when you last followed up
Runs a standard Google search and summarizes any red flags in a fixed format
Pulls the CRM export from email and drafts the monthly report
Watch out for
Make sure the skill reads the whole thread, not just the latest reply
Common names; use only the news sources the firm has approved
Sensitive project data; keep the export limited to the fields the report needs
